Privacy Policy

Jom BM (com.bahasamelayu)
Effective date: 11 October 2026

Jom BM is a Malay practice app. This policy explains what the app stores on your phone and what it sends to other services when you use it.

Account

You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Google. Firebase Authentication, a Google service, handles the sign-in. The app receives your account identifier, email address, and the name you enter or the name from your Google account. The app does not receive your Google password.

Signing out removes the session from the app. It does not delete the Firebase account.

Conversations and practice

When you chat, translate a sentence, or look up a word, the text you enter is sent to Google’s Gemini API so the app can reply or translate. Short notes about you, such as your name or something you said you like, may also be sent to Gemini so later conversations can use them. Those notes are then saved on your device.

Word lookups may also be sent to Glosbe and Wiktionary. Those requests contain the word or phrase you looked up.

Chat history, vocabulary notes, match-game progress, and the learner notes are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes that local data.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you start voice input. The recording is passed to the speech recognizer on your device. On Android, that recognizer is usually Google’s speech service, which may receive the audio in order to turn it into text. The app does not keep the audio.

Courses you publish

If you publish a conversation as a course, its title and messages are saved in Google Cloud Firestore. Other people who use Jom BM can open that course. Do not publish a conversation that contains information you want to keep private.

What this app does not do

  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not sell your information.
  • It does not use an analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Children

Jom BM is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you remove it in the app or uninstall Jom BM. Account data stays in Firebase until you ask for the account to be deleted. A course you publish stays in Firestore until it is removed.

Your choices

You can refuse the microphone permission and still type. You can sign out from the menu. To delete your Firebase account, or to ask about a published course, email the address below.
